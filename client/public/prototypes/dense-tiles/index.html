Most owners can’t say exactly what’s slowing them down, and they shouldn’t have to. It hides in plain sight: the same details typed twice, the voicemails nobody wrote down, the invoices that sit until someone remembers. Zeke starts with a conversation about how your week really goes. We listen for the jobs that repeat, the ones that eat an afternoon and need a person only because nobody built anything better. Then we build the fix around the way you already work. You don’t write up your problem. You talk to us, and we find it.

How it works

You don’t need a plan, a list of requirements or the right words. You need a conversation. Here is what happens after it.

Zeke asks how your week really goes: where the calls land, what gets typed twice, what waits until Friday. You talk; he takes the notes.

We sort what we heard into the jobs that repeat, and pick the one that gives you the most time back first.

Ben and Zeke build the automation or AI tool around the way you already work, and show it to you working on your own examples.

The work arrives done. You check it, and get on with your day.

  • The call-back list is there in the morning
  • The invoices are ready to approve
  • The reminders went out last night
  • Friday’s numbers are already in your inbox

Questions

What if I don’t know what to automate?

That’s normal, and it’s why we start with a conversation instead of a form. Tell us how your week goes. Finding the busywork is our job.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Small businesses of any kind: trades, clinics, offices, shops, salons, firms. If a job happens every day or every week, there is usually something to build.

Do I have to be near Nashville?

No. We’re near Nashville and glad to work with neighbors, but we work by phone, Zoom, Teams or Google Meet with businesses anywhere.

Will I have to change the software I use?

We start from the way you already work. The point is to take work off your plate, not to hand you a new system to learn.

What’s an AI tool, in plain words?

A small program that can read, sort or write the way a person would: turn a voicemail into a call-back note, draft a reply, pull the totals out of an invoice. We use it where it saves time and skip it where a plain automation does the job.

Who will I actually talk to?

Zeke or Ben, the two people who build it. There’s nobody in between.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what we find and what we build, so there’s no price list. Once we know what’s slowing you down, we’ll walk you through what the fix involves.

Will this replace my staff?

It takes the copying, chasing and re-typing off their plate, so their day goes to the parts of the job that need a person.
