How it works

  1. First, we talk

    A conversation by phone, Zoom, Teams or Google Meet. You tell us how a normal week goes. No list needed.

  2. Then we find the busywork

    We map how the work really moves: which inquiries wait too long, what gets typed twice, which job eats Friday afternoon.

  3. We build the fix

    We agree on what to build and what it costs, then build it around the email, spreadsheets and software you already use.

  4. Less to do by hand

    Inquiries get answered, quotes get followed up, reminders go out. You keep the parts that need you, like the customers.

Questions

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Small businesses that want to grow: shops, studios, salons, offices, cafés and more. If someone types the same thing twice, follows up the same people every week or copies numbers from one screen to another, there’s usually something to build.

Do you only work around Nashville?

We’re based in Nashville, but we’re glad to work with anybody, because technology has brought the world together. We talk by phone, Zoom, Teams or Google Meet, wherever you are.

Will we have to switch software?

We start from what you already use: your email, your spreadsheets, your accounting or booking software. The point is less for you to learn, not more.

Where does AI come in?

Some jobs need judgment, not just rules: reading an email and deciding what it’s about, summarizing a phone call, pulling figures off a PDF, drafting a reply for you to check. That’s where an AI tool fits. Where a plain rule does the job, we use the plain rule.

What does it cost?

It depends on what we build, so there’s no price list here. The first conversation is free. Before you commit, we tell you what we’d build, what it costs, and the monthly cost of looking after it, and we work with you to find a version that fits your budget.

What happens after it’s built?

We look after it. If something isn’t working right, tell us.

What happens to my data?

It stays yours. The tools we build use it only to do the job you asked for. We don’t look through it or use it for anything else.
