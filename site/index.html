IT, and the first conversation

Zeke usually has the first talk with you and works out how your work really happens before anything gets built.

  1. First, we talk

    A conversation by phone, Zoom, Teams or Meet, usually with Zeke. You walk us through a normal week. No list needed.

  2. Then we find the busywork

    We map how the work really moves: what gets typed twice, who gets chased, which job eats Friday afternoon.

  3. We build the fix

    A custom automation or AI tool, built around the email, spreadsheets and software you already use.

  4. You stop doing it by hand

    The call-backs get listed, the invoices get drafted, the reminders go out. You keep the parts that need you.

Questions

We don’t know what to automate. Is that a problem?

No. That’s the normal starting point, and finding it is our job. You walk us through how a week goes, and we come back with what we’d take off your plate.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Any kind: shops, trades, salons, offices, clinics, churches, nonprofits. If someone types the same thing twice, chases the same people every week or copies numbers from one screen to another, there’s usually something to build.

Do you only work around Nashville?

We’re based near Nashville and glad to work with Middle Tennessee businesses, but everything happens by phone, Zoom, Teams or Google Meet, so you can be anywhere.

Will we have to switch software?

We start from what you already use: your email, your spreadsheets, your accounting or booking software. The point is less for you to learn, not more.

Where does AI come in?

Some jobs need judgment, not just rules: reading an email and deciding what it’s about, summarising a phone call, pulling figures off a PDF, drafting a reply for you to check. That’s where an AI tool fits. Where a plain rule does the job, we use the plain rule.

What does it cost?

It depends on what we find, so there’s no price list on this page. Cost is part of the first conversation, along with what we’d build.

Who will we actually talk to?

Ben or Zeke. There are two of us, so the people you talk to are the people who build it.
