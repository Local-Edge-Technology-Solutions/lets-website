Questions

We don’t know what to automate. Is that a problem?

No. That’s the normal starting point, and finding it is our job. You tell us how a week goes, and we come back with what we’d take off your plate.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Small businesses that want to grow: shops, studios, salons, offices, cafés and more. If someone types the same thing twice, follows up the same people every week or copies numbers from one screen to another, there’s usually something to build.

Do you only work around Nashville?

We’re based in Nashville, but we’re glad to work with anybody, because technology has brought the world together. We talk by phone, Zoom, Teams or Google Meet, wherever you are.

Will we have to switch software?

We start from what you already use: your email, your spreadsheets, your accounting or booking software. The point is less for you to learn, not more.

Where does AI come in?

Some jobs need judgment, not just rules: reading an email and deciding what it’s about, summarising a phone call, pulling figures off a PDF, drafting a reply for you to check. That’s where an AI tool fits. Where a plain rule does the job, we use the plain rule.

What does it cost?

It depends on what we build, so there’s no price list here. The first conversation is about how your business works. Once we know what to build, we’ll tell you what it costs and work with you to find a version that fits your budget.

Who will we actually talk to?

Ben and Zeke. There are two of us, and we’re the people who build it.
